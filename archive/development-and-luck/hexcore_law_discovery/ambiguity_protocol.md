# DEV-08: post-run identifiability audit

Specified after DEV-05 results were seen; exploratory follow-up. Enumerate the quotient of the same finite candidate profiles by their entire permitted transcript n=4..16. Because sizes 2,3 are fixed by clipping, no further training query can split a class. Calculate number of singleton classes, ambiguous profiles, largest class, and the optimal exact-law identification accuracy under a uniform prior on distinct candidate profiles (one successful profile per class). All distinct profiles in a class disagree somewhere in the held-out domain. Compare classes for the six in-language reference worlds. For the three deliberately aliased evaluator worlds, check equality of all permitted evidence and pairwise inequality of held-out root predictions at every size. This establishes an observation-interface impossibility rather than a search failure. Also compare active and passive measured candidate visits and fit time; report the simpler fraction fit and the same-language passive solver as ordinary baselines. No additional training oracle or new candidate laws.
