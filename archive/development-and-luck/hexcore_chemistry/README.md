# Hexcore: finite term chemistry and inheritance (exploratory)

Run `python chemistry.py`, then `python followup.py` (Python 3.10+ and NumPy). Original protocols were written before each run; the follow-up was designed after observing the initial failure. JSON includes per-seed results, summaries and the initial target formulas. Timings depend on host. The work proxy charges expanded formula operations but is not physical energy or a complete computation/storage accounting. The evaluator tracks previously generated functions externally, and this history is not made available to the random pools. Generated coverage does not mean simultaneously retained capability. Actual resident hit counts are separately reported. Neither program implements natural selection, membranes, metabolic autocatalysis or endogenous primitive-law discovery. The food, NAND and substitution rules are supplied. This is bounded program search used as a toy chemistry, not an independently validated model of abiogenesis.

## Main outcome

24 seeds, six search variants. Every variant's mean target coverage decreased under inheritance when the vocabulary expanded from four inputs to five. Template random: 15.33 inherited vs 19.25 cold (paired mean difference -3.92; descriptive bootstrap 95% interval [-6.04,-1.75]), vs 33.08 cold at equal lifetime work. No parity5 or majority5 hits. Average target discovery in the template arm exceeds the tested NAND-only and subtree random variants, but this uses a hand-supplied substitution operation and an in-grammar task distribution.

The follow-up tested input relabeling, leaf scrambling, randomized tree shapes, partial turnover, small inherited subtrees, and size-matched random small trees. Small inherited fragments do not outperform matched random fragments in either tested arm (template -0.29, CI [-2.96,2.29]; subtree +0.04, CI [-2.13,2.21]). No inherited condition beats cold at equal lifetime work. These are exploratory results, not proof of the absence of useful inheritance. Same phase-two budget does not match lifetime cost; full-budget cold starts also have earlier access to the new input. Actual compute overhead, independent verification of every discovered solution, persistent pool encoding and alternative stronger search baselines remain incomplete.

512 held-out target truth tables independently checked with scalar evaluation over all 32 assignments. 144 repeated hit-count comparisons across the original and follow-up conditions matched. All follow-up work budgets checked. Historical results preserved unedited.
