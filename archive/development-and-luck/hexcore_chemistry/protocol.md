# Exploratory protocol: compressing architectural discovery
Frozen before initial execution. Finite Boolean term chemistry, not physical chemistry or a neural net. Primitive laws and food supplied. No target labels, task reward, fitness, explicit promotion, reproduction, or reasoning enter random-pool dynamics. Every term can be an operand and (in template arms) a rule for another term.

Postfix NAND formulae, initially four inputs. Fully expanded trees (no free macros or DAG sharing). Food bank: all inputs and unordered NAND pairs, including repeats. 64 pool slots, maximum 31 NAND gates (63 symbols) per term. Sample each operand from food with probability 0.2, otherwise pool. NAND-only combines operands under NAND. Template: half the time replace all occurrences of a randomly chosen input port in first operand by second operand; otherwise NAND. Subtree: half the time replace one uniformly sampled syntactic subtree; otherwise NAND (ordinary GP). Two retention policies per reaction law: random overwrite, or semantic library that keeps unique truth tables and the shorter duplicate, otherwise randomly evicts when full. The latter is a supplied heuristic. Random pools start full of sampled food, semantic pools start with unique food and fill empty slots.

Charge len(A)+len(B)+1 per proposal plus 2*len(product) for in-cap products (materialization and truth-table evaluation). Oversize proposals rejected; allocation and bookkeeping are not fully captured, so report actual timing as well. Reject last proposal if it exceeds budget. All expanded gates and occupied token counts recorded. This is a work proxy, not energy or FLOPs.

24 seeds, 160,000 work units per phase per arm. First phase four inputs, count unique generated truth tables and full-support functions. Second phase adds fifth input; compare inherited pools with cold reset pools under same phase-two budget and paired seeds. Neither sees evaluation targets. External test suite: 512 distinct independently seeded five-input NAND functions generated by random trees (2–12 gates), each depending on fifth input and at least three old inputs; two controls parity5 and majority5. Score exact generated/resident hits, diversity, gate sizes, work/time. Target generator shares grammar, so no out-of-domain claim. Primary contrast warm vs cold template random; compare template to NAND and ordinary subtree search, semantic libraries. Also run cold with full two-phase budget for lifetime comparison. Report paired bootstrap intervals (descriptive, exploratory; no multiplicity correction). Preserve failures. No claim of emergent individuality, reasoning, changing physical laws or novel mathematics.
