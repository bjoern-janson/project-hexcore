## Research implications (hypotheses, not promotions)

1. Distinguish causal participation, identifiable participation, predictive utility and explicit self-representation.
2. Integration and persistence can eliminate access to contrary evidence. The rule deciding when to integrate is itself a candidate for correction.
3. Learned instruments can reduce search or verification cost in structured ecologies, but inherited structure can conceal new failures.
4. Computational equivalence of an external behavior is not automatically equivalence of internal causal organization; no consciousness implication follows.
5. A common mutable medium is a new experimental object. None of these results demonstrates its viability, emergent reasoning, sentience, autonomy, general intelligence, or open-ended self-improvement.

The correct next experiment is governed by the proposed [medium specification](../docs/MEDIUM.md) and [protocol](../docs/PROTOCOL.md), not by a desire to upgrade this archive. Strong ordinary controls, matched costs, causally discriminating ablations and fresh held-out evidence remain required.
