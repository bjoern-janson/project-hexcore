# Chat research archive: C0 and Bitter Lesson (November 2026)

Status: HISTORICAL / EXPLORATORY / NOT INDEPENDENTLY REPLAYED.

These two lineages precede or motivate Project Hexcore's proposed common mutable medium. They are not experiments of that medium, a new scientific promotion, or a replacement for the existing [42-experiment lineage](../docs/LINEAGE.md). Numbering is local to each conversation: C0–C0p contains 16 numbered assays; BL-01–BL-09 contains nine, including multiple controls and variants. Together with the earlier 42 this is 67 numbered exploratory experiments, not 67 independent confirmed results.

The original July question is preserved in [Consciousness as a Self-Integrating Search Process](https://github.com/bjoern-janson/computational-phase-boundary/blob/62d10dc632554df9c69d50fd913d8e16e3eb45a4/docs/consciousness.md). The chat returned to causal mass, then examined causal attribution, self-models, distributed organization and the economics of discovery and correction. The successive generic controls repeatedly narrowed the claims.
