Source: user-provided quotation in the November 2026 research conversation; summarized here rather than represented as an independent scientific review.

## Response and disposition
Accept the legibility and fixed-instrument requirements. Preserve the earlier negative results. Distinguish energetic, epistemic and corrective constraints. Stopping is not correction; destroying a medium is not evidence of repairability. A finite simulator still has a declared transition law, though exact closure may be infeasible. Do not upgrade behavior to certified reachability without a suitable instrument. Treat interference as a possible source of testable pressure for causal discrimination, not a guaranteed route to cognition.
