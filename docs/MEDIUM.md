# Candidate medium — specification boundary

Status: design proposal, not implemented in full. A future executable protocol must freeze primitive transition equations, scheduler, seeds, resource ledger and observables before evaluation.

## Minimal prescriptions
1. Local causal interaction.
2. Mutable relationships: interactions modify conditions of later interactions.
3. Explicit finite resource accounting for activity, storage, rewiring and persistence.
4. External coupling: emissions affect an independently controlled environment; echoes return.
5. Dissipation: organization is not preserved for free.

No explicit organisms, fitness, reproduction, memory registers, learning schedule, novelty detector, reasoning engine or designated meta-controller in the target medium. This is an aspiration; every implemented exception must be recorded. The simulator still has fixed laws, even if its internal state/rule distinction dissolves.

## Immutable experimental boundary
The evaluator owns world transitions, budgets, intervention ports, metrics, random seeds, stopping conditions and logs. No simulated structure may modify these. A valve is a stop mechanism, not proof of correctability. Energy constrains persistence, not truth. Epistemic accessibility, energetic feasibility and corrective causal access must be measured separately.

## Hypotheses, not results
Memory as persistent recurrent activity; computation as structured propagation; learning as changing interactions; meta-adaptation as changing conditions of change. Spatial topology may generate temporal diversity without explicit fast/slow labels. Multiple interacting processes may generate endogenous selection pressures and interference. Distinguishing self-caused echoes from other causes may be useful, but no claim of selfhood follows.

## Inspiration
Jazz: coordination and improvisation. Echolocation: active probes constrained by external responses. Non-equilibrium dynamics: sustained organization under resource flux. DeepDream/psychedelic imagery: endogenous amplification and recursive motifs, not a mechanistic equivalence. Flow: uninterrupted coupling, not a demonstrated thermodynamic signature. Psychiatric analogy: generic rigidity, salience, fragmentation and correction failure are simulation axes, not a theory or diagnosis of schizophrenia.

Constant flux means persistent organization through change, not indiscriminate randomness. No claim of physical thermodynamics until actual energy laws and dissipation are modeled and tested.
