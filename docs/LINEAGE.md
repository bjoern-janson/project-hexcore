## Why mechanisms were removed
Explicit search failed on combinatorial growth; specialization failed under changed query relevance; recursive control failed on cost and inconsistent state; ecological selection failed when too harsh or too forgiving; reinforcement froze the medium; fixed timescales failed across environments. Removing a mechanism does not remove the need to test the failure it exposed.

## Research fingerprints and open questions
Correction cost under bounded memory, scope-dependent authority, common-mode failure, representation discovery, transferable impossibility, endogenous temporal organization, and sustained causal access to mechanisms of change. Public indexing or conceptual similarities do not establish influence on frontier AI training.
