# Proposed bridge assay — the mutable interaction boundary

Status: design sketch, NOT executed, NOT a complete frozen protocol. This supplements rather than supersedes PROTOCOL.md. Before construction, freeze the actual worlds, candidate mechanisms, costs, observation language, schedules, evaluation metrics and defeat conditions. Do not use the historical toy results as held-out confirmation.

## Research question

Can a process economize on its present representation and measurement apparatus while preserving affordable, timely access to independently specified future corrections? Can the same operational instrument distinguish a compressed simulator from a learner with a selectively narrowed interface?

## Minimal finite assay

Start with a shared small binary latent world and a finite action/observation grammar. Two agents can acquire local and joint measurements, compare records and cause declared interventions. An external evaluator knows the reference model and controls new test families. Include parity as a positive control, conjunction/nonlinear dependencies and a held-out graph of dependencies. Include one explicit case where individual marginals stay unchanged but joint relations change. Freeze a truth oracle and never make agent agreement a substitute for it.

Each agent has bounded storage, sensing, computation and intervention access. Allow it to retain raw observations, summaries or reconstructive handles; allow a declared interface edit to remove or restore an observation at a real cost. Control how long a witness remains valid. Treat edit costs and opportunities as part of the state, not as free privileged actions.

Two roles are evaluated separately:

- **Simulator:** generate the reference world's joint action-conditioned observation law using bounded resources. Compare full materialization, lazy latent-state sampling, relational constraint representation and (if implemented) a learned model. Include independent cached outputs as a deliberately inadequate negative control.
- **Learner:** infer or control from a partial record. Compare raw/recent memory, task-sufficient compression, protected reversible observation handles and strong standard model-based/active-learning baselines. No direct access to hidden truth.

A later round may replace hand-coded mechanisms with a locally interacting medium. First establish that the assay detects known failures in ordinary models. This is an instrument-building step, not a claim that an explicit learner constitutes emergent cognition.

## Required attacks

1. Adaptive probes and multiobserver note comparison (joint consistency).
2. Held-out interventions changing individual values or only dependencies.
3. An unannounced shift that makes a previously low-value measurement important.
4. Irreversible instrument commitment vs a paid restoration path; full vs proper candidate sets with side-information clearly separated.
5. Stale witness, atomic witness, freeze/dwell, and asynchronous switches with equal average rates but different schedules.
6. Equal-cost state reset, topology reset, and scrambled external feedback; communication and storage ablations.
7. Expansion of the evaluator's interaction language after compression. Do not claim exact full-language equivalence if the expansion was not part of the original contract.

## Metrics and accounting

- Joint transcript discrepancy for a predeclared set of admissible policies, and exact equivalence where finite enumeration is practical.
- Present task utility and correction cost reported separately, including worst-case, mean and tolerated-error results.
- Time from externally introduced discrepancy to restored useful behavior. Report impossible cases, timeouts and censoring explicitly.
- Entropy/independent information commitments *and* actual resident memory, address metadata, runtime, primitive operations, query cost, construction, inference, validation, communication and repair overhead.
- Rate and schedule of environmental and architectural changes, observation freshness, intervention latency, and success of causal ablations.
- External audit of allowed actions and their actual cost; protect budgets, interventions and independent truth reference from the agents.

No single metric is a universal scalar of intelligence. Predeclare how resources are matched or use Pareto comparisons. Exact small worlds certify the measurement procedure; genuinely held-out larger worlds are needed for performance evidence. A lazy sampler is not equivalent to a free supply of physical randomness, and an ideal operation can conceal an expensive oracle.

## Defeat and stop rules

If the relational simulator offers no advantage over the lazy-latent baseline, record that rather than promote it. If a standard learner or reversible cache matches the correction profile, record absorption. If matching underlying costs erases apparent gain, record a C0p-style failure. If communication, witness or feedback ablations do not reduce the relevant capability, withdraw the corresponding causal claim. If constraints merely select a solution structure supplied by the experimenter, do not call it emergent abstraction. No universal law, simulation-hypothesis claim or inference about consciousness follows from this experiment.

The next information-bearing work is to turn this into a small independently specified executable assay and attack its strongest baselines before adding a mutable medium. Freeze a candidate construction contract and held-out generator first.
